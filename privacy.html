Privacy Policy

One Plus Fashion ERP · Last updated: 18 October 2026

One Plus Fashion ERP ("the app") is a business-management program that runs entirely in your web browser. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it is kept.

1. Where your data is stored

  • On your computer. Everything you enter (customers, suppliers, invoices, payments, stock and settings) is saved in your browser's storage on that computer.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you sign in with Google. The app saves a copy of that data in a folder named "One Plus Fashion ERP" in the Google Drive of the account you signed in with, so that it can be restored on another computer.

The app has no server of its own. We (the developer) do not receive, collect, store or see your business data, and nothing is sent anywhere except to your own Google Drive.

2. Google account data the app uses

When you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks Google for one permission: to see, edit, create and delete only the Google Drive files it creates (drive.file). The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.

The app also reads the signed-in account's email address and name from Google Drive, only to show on this computer which account the backup goes to.

The app uses this access only to save, update and read back its own data file, its dated daily backup copies, and any "Conflict copy" files it makes when two computers changed the data at the same time.

The sign-in permission (an access token) is kept only in the open browser tab and expires after about an hour. The app does not keep your Google password: you type it only on Google's own sign-in page.

3. Limited use of Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not read by people.

4. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.
  • No access to any Google Drive file the app did not create.

5. Your choices

  • Stop backing up: open Settings / Backup in the app and click Sign out.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove "One Plus Fashion ERP".
  • Delete the data in Google Drive: delete the "One Plus Fashion ERP" folder in your Drive.
  • Delete the data on a computer: use Settings / Backup → Clear All Data in the app, or clear the browser's site data.

6. Security

Data travels between your browser and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Keep your Google account and your computer secure: anyone who can use them can open the data.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
